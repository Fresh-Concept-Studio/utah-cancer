A Study of ART0380 for the Treatment of Advanced or Metastatic Solid TumorsOpen
Protocol: NCT04657068

This study is testing ART0380, an oral investigational cancer drug, in people with advanced or metastatic solid tumors. Researchers want to find safe doses, understand side effects, and learn whether ART0380 may work alone or with gemcitabine or irinotecan. Some study groups are focused on tumors with specific DNA repair changes, including certain ovarian and related gynecologic cancers.

Elacestrant Versus Standard Endocrine Therapy in Women and Men With Node-positive, Estrogen Receptor-positive, HER2-negative, Early Breast Cancer With High Risk of RecurrenceOpen
Protocol: NCT06492616

This phase 3 study is comparing elacestrant with standard endocrine therapy in women and men with early breast cancer that is ER-positive, HER2-negative, node-positive, and at high risk of recurrence. Participants must have already received endocrine therapy and may have previously received certain targeted treatments. The study is recruiting at multiple sites.

Palazestrant in Combination With Ribociclib for the First-line Treatment of ER+/HER2- Advanced Breast CancerOpen
Protocol: NCT07085767

This phase 3 study is comparing palazestrant plus ribociclib with letrozole plus ribociclib for adults with ER-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer. It is enrolling women and men who have not received prior systemic anti-cancer treatment for advanced disease. Participants will be randomly assigned to one of the two treatment arms in a double-blind design.

Metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer

A Study of Pasritamig Versus Placebo in Late Line Metastatic Castration-resistant Prostate Cancer (mCRPC)Open
Protocol: NCT07164443

This phase 3 study is testing pasritamig (JNJ-78278343) plus best supportive care versus placebo plus best supportive care in people with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer. The main goal is to see whether pasritamig helps people live longer. It is recruiting participants at multiple centers in the United States.

Metastatic colorectal cancer

Phase III Study of Ivonescimab or Bevacizumab Combined With FOLFOX in Patients With Metastatic Colorectal CancerOpen
Protocol: NCT07228832

This phase 3 study is testing whether ivonescimab plus FOLFOX works better than bevacizumab plus FOLFOX as a first treatment for metastatic colorectal cancer. The trial is randomized, double-blind, and active-controlled, and it includes about 600 participants who have not previously received systemic therapy for metastatic disease. Treatment is given intravenously, followed by maintenance therapy for up to 2 years.

Expanded Access Program to Treat Patients With Previously Treated Metastatic Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma With DaraxonrasibOpen
Protocol: NCT07573215

This expanded access program may provide daraxonrasib to adults with previously treated metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma who have no comparable or satisfactory alternative treatment options. It is intended for patients who cannot join an ongoing daraxonrasib clinical trial because of ineligibility or lack of access. Daraxonrasib is supplied by the sponsor free of charge during treatment under this program.

Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

Clinical Study of Ivonescimab for First-line Treatment of Metastatic NSCLC Patients With High PD-L1Open
Protocol: NCT06767514

This phase 3 study is testing ivonescimab versus pembrolizumab as a first treatment for people with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer whose tumors have high PD-L1 expression. The study is looking at outcomes such as overall survival and progression-free survival. Participants must be adults with stage IV NSCLC and no prior systemic treatment for metastatic disease.

A Phase II/III, Multisite, Randomized Master Protocol for a Global Trial of BNT327 in Combination With Chemotherapy and Other Investigational Agents in First-line Non-Small Cell Lung CancerOpen
Protocol: NCT06712316

This is a phase 2/3, multicenter study for people with first-line non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Participants are randomized to receive BNT327 (pumitamig) with chemotherapy, and the study includes two substudies based on tumor subtype. The trial is recruiting and is designed to evaluate safety, effectiveness, and pharmacokinetics.

Ovarian cancer

A Phase 2, Single-Arm Study of INCB123667 in Participants With Platinum-Resistant Ovarian Cancer With Cyclin E1 Overexpression (MAESTRA 1)Open
Protocol: NCT07023627

This phase 2 study is evaluating INCB123667 in people with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer that overexpresses Cyclin E1. The study is looking at the safety and effectiveness of this treatment in participants who have already received prior therapy. It is a single-arm study, so all participants receive the study drug.

Study of Trastuzumab Deruxtecan With Bevacizumab Versus Bevacizumab Monotherapy for First-line Maintenance in HER2-Expressing Ovarian Cancer (DESTINY-Ovarian01)Open
Protocol: NCT06819007

This phase 3 trial is evaluating trastuzumab deruxtecan plus bevacizumab versus bevacizumab alone as first-line maintenance treatment for HER2-expressing advanced high-grade ovarian cancer. Participants must have newly diagnosed stage III or IV epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal cancer and have already received standard bevacizumab with platinum-based chemotherapy. A safety run-in phase is planned before randomization.

Pancreatic cancer

A Phase 3 Study of Setidegrasib With mFOLFIRINOX or NALIRIFOX in KRAS G12D Mutated Metastatic Pancreatic AdenocarcinomaOpen
Protocol: NCT07409272

This phase 3 study is testing whether setidegrasib, given with standard chemotherapy, can help people with metastatic pancreatic cancer that has a KRAS G12D mutation. Participants are assigned by chance to receive setidegrasib or placebo along with mFOLFIRINOX or NALIRIFOX, which the study doctor selects. The main goal is to see whether the study treatment helps people live longer and to assess safety.
